frag-8: page-disclaimer + prose-sentence-shard screens (weaknesses)

Two new fragment screens in fragment_item_reasons, both weaknesses-only,
wired through the shared filter_list_field_items_detailed funnel so they
fire in recovery and repair paths.

page_disclaimer: first-person voice AND a strong disclaimer/attribution
phrase ("not my own", "my fault", "message me", "this page", "their
videos", "in my opinion", ...), plus a capped-name safety net for
"displayed on any profile". Calibrated: 4 raw / 2 distinct hits in the
stored weakness corpus (Discord note x3, Death Battle disclaimer x1);
cap artifacts (darkseid "was stated" + cut-off "i", momo "My Hero
Academia") pinned as non-firing controls.

prose_sentence_shard: leading-edge continuation markers -- conjunction
(but/and/so/yet/however/although/though/rather than), preposition
(during/after/before/with/without/by/through/despite/because of/due to),
or gerund (-ing openers with a denylist), or a trailing lead-in
("for example"/"such as"/"for instance"). Spares: subject-led finite
clauses (with contraction expansion), comma + main clause, limitation
markers anywhere, "without" + noun phrase. Calibrated: 4,281 raw / 388
distinct openers. The darwin lead clause ("His body will adapt ...")
is a complete clause and is deliberately NOT caught -- sibling-cluster
detection was calibrated and rejected (4,951 raw / 2,322 distinct, hits
real limitation content); the gate decides whether it re-merges.

Fixes found by calib3 verbatim sanity: _shard_headed now uses only
auxiliaries + 3rd-person -s verbs (-ed/irregular-past belong only in
the subject-led spare; "combat-related power" was misread as headed),
and _SHARD_FUNCTION gained a noun-head set (determiners/quantifiers/
adjectives) so plural nouns ("many situations") are not misread as
verbs. All four verbatim shards now fire; all controls survive.
