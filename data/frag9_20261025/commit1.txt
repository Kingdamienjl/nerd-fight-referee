frag-9: relative-pronoun + or/even/like + numeric-split + header-cutoff screens (weaknesses)

Four new fragment screens in fragment_item_reasons (weaknesses only,
shared funnel, so they fire in recovery and repair paths), plus a
thousands-comma guard in split_listish (harvester root cause for the
numeric split). All 10 gate-50l verbatim items drop; all controls
survive; guard 213/213; full discover 519 tests at 13F + 11E,
byte-identical to the pre-existing band, zero new guard/demotion
failures.

relative_pronoun_opener: an item opening with which/that/who/whom/
whose/what is a continuation of a sentence whose head stayed on the
page ("which has causes drastic shifts in his personality.", "which
can weaken or nullify the suit's healing powers."). The frag-8
headed-clause spare does NOT apply here -- the relative pronoun is
itself the subject of its clause, so "which bounces..." looks headed
while still being a fragment with no antecedent. An item is whole
only if it carries a MAIN clause of its own (after a comma, or after
a sentence boundary). No limitation spare: the relative clause's own
verb ("weaken") does not make the item whole. One spare, and it is
load-bearing: a PAST-TENSE relative clause narrates a completed event
(a documented exploit), which the frag-5 long-clause calibration
deliberately protects -- "which allowed Denji to successfully ambush
her in their final confrontation" is a pinned zero-reason control, so
the screen spares relative clauses whose main verb is past tense
(-ed, irregular past, was/were/had). The gate-50l items are
present-tense/modal property statements and still fire. Calibrated:
869 raw hits (which 696, that 79, who 49, whom 18, what 16, whose
11); sampled hits are fragment-class throughout.

prose_sentence_shard extensions (same reason, same spares): "or"
joins the conjunction set (it was simply missing in frag-8 -- the
gate-50l venom item "or by anything that can pass through the
symbiote..." slipped through on exactly that gap); "like" joins the
preposition set ("like all others of its kind"); "even" is a new
adverb-opener class ("even in the eminence of a battle."). Two
fixes found by the failing verbatim test: _shard_subject_led no
longer counts a relative pronoun attached to a preceding noun as a
main-clause subject ("anything that can pass..." -- "that" is a
relative pronoun there, not the subject of a main clause); "even
without" + a noun phrase is spared like "without" + a noun phrase
("even without her horn" is a real limitation statement).
Calibrated: 394 raw hits via the new openers (or 235, like 97, even
62); sampled hits are fragment-class throughout.

numeric_split_fragment + split_listish guard: "radiation up to
4,000 hertz" was split at its thousands comma into "radiation up to
4" / "000 hertz". Root cause: split_listish splits on every comma.
It now skips thousands-separator commas (digit before, exactly three
digits after, non-digit after that) -- ordinary list commas
("Strength 4, Speed 5", "1,000, 2,000") still split. Backstop
screen: an item opening with exactly three digits (fourth character
not a digit) and carrying no clause of its own is a split half or an
image-size fragment. Calibrated: 397 raw / 100 distinct (dominated
by 400px/300px/250px image-size fragments); the limitation spare
protects real numeric limits ("180 degree blind spot"). No rule on
items ENDING in a bare digit: 1,172 such items, including legit
"Season 3" / "White & Red #3" shapes.

header_cutoff: "Header: <rest>" where the rest is cut off --
subordinate-only rest (As/If/When/... with no main clause after a
comma and no limitation marker; the subordinate check runs BEFORE
the headed check because "If Anti-Venom overuses his healing
powers" has a finite verb inside the subordinate clause), shard
rest (the post-colon text itself trips the prose-shard screens:
"Through the Force", "Drawing power from the moon", "Like all
Ainur"), or a short definite-NP rest ("The " + 2-4 words: "The
Venom symbiote"). A whole rest survives: subject-led finite clause,
main clause after a comma, leading finite verb ("Burns on contact"),
or a limitation marker anywhere; longer definitions survive too
("Ki Blast: The most basic form of energy wave."). "A "/"An "
definitions (187 items) and the 1,610-item other-bare-NP class are
deliberately NOT caught -- accepted residual, the gate decides.
Calibrated: 529 raw (subordinate_only 319, shard_rest 203,
the_np_short 7).
